Before making changes:

1. Inspect the existing repository.
2. Do not overwrite working code unnecessarily.
3. Do not delete files unless explicitly required.
4. Do not change public output schemas without explaining why.
5. Do not add dependencies unless necessary.
6. Do not introduce frameworks just for convenience.
7. Do not hard-code dataset-specific artist IDs.
8. Do not hard-code expected winners.
9. Do not manually encode recommendation results.
10. Do not fabricate missing media evidence.
11. Run tests after meaningful changes.
12. Show a concise change summary.
13. Preserve reproducibility.
14. Keep the implementation simple enough for a 6-hour assignment.

If uncertain, inspect first rather than guessing.
